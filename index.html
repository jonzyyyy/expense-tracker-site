Sift

A private expense tracker for iPhone that keeps your money data on your phone — not on someone's server.

Coming soon to the App Store

What it does

  • Scan receiptsShare any receipt photo straight into Sift. The total, merchant and date are read on your device and filled in for you.
  • Import statementsBring in a PDF statement and Sift pulls out the transactions — no re-typing a month of spending.
  • Budgets that make senseSet limits per category and see where you actually stand, not just where you've been.
  • Split billsTrack who owes what after a group meal or trip, and settle up when the money lands.
  • Spot subscriptionsSift notices recurring charges, so the gym membership you forgot about stops being invisible.
  • Any currencySpend abroad and amounts convert at the rate on the day of the transaction.
  • Log it by voiceAsk Siri, tap the Action Button, or use a Home Screen shortcut to record a payment in seconds.
  • Trends worth readingSee how spending moves across months and categories, filtered however you like.

Your data stays yours

Sift has no account, no sign-in, and no analytics. Every transaction, receipt and statement is stored and processed on your device only. Nothing is uploaded, sold, or shared — there is no server to upload it to.

The single network request Sift ever makes is a currency-rate lookup, and it sends only a date and two currency codes. Never an amount, a merchant, or anything identifying you. Read the full privacy policy.
