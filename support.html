Support

Where is my data stored?

Entirely on your device. Expense Tracker has no account system and no server. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Will my data sync between devices?

Not currently. Data stays on the device where it was entered. Deleting the app deletes its data, so back up your device if you want to keep it.

Adding a transaction from a receipt

Open the photo of your receipt in the Photos app, tap the Share button, and choose Add from Receipt. The receipt is read on your device and used to pre-fill the transaction, which you can edit before saving.

Logging payments automatically from notifications

This is optional and set up through Apple's Shortcuts app. Instructions are in the app under Settings. The app cannot read notifications on its own — it only receives text that a Shortcuts automation you create explicitly passes to it.

Foreign-currency transactions

Amounts in another currency are converted using the European Central Bank reference rate for the transaction's date. Rates are published on business days, so weekend and holiday dates use the most recent prior business day. If the lookup fails, the original amount and currency are still saved.
