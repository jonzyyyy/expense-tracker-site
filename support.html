Support

Questions, bug reports, and feature requests are welcome by email: siftapp.support@gmail.com.

Where is my data stored?

Entirely on your device. Sift has no account system and no server. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Will my data sync between devices?

Not currently. Data stays on the device where it was entered. Deleting the app deletes its data, so back up your device if you want to keep it.

Adding a transaction from a receipt

Tap + on the Home tab and choose Add from Receipt to take a photo or pick one. Or open the photo in the Photos app, tap the Share button, and choose Add from Receipt. The receipt is read on your device and used to pre-fill the transaction, which you can edit before saving.

Logging payments automatically

Apple Pay. Purchases made with Apple Pay can be logged automatically using the Wallet automation in Apple's Shortcuts app. It's optional, and step-by-step instructions are in the app under Settings → Automation. Sift only receives what that automation passes to it.

PayNow and other bank transfers. These don't go through Apple Pay, and iOS doesn't let apps read bank notifications. Take a screenshot of the payment confirmation in your bank's app and add it with Add from Receipt — the amount and payee are filled in for you.

Foreign-currency transactions

Amounts in another currency are converted using the European Central Bank reference rate for the transaction's date. Rates are published on business days, so weekend and holiday dates use the most recent prior business day. If the lookup fails, the original amount and currency are still saved.
