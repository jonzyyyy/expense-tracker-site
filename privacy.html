Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 July 2026

Expense Tracker is a personal finance app that runs entirely on your device. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

Summary. Expense Tracker does not collect, transmit, or store your personal or financial data on any server. There is no account, no login, and no analytics. Your transactions never leave your device.

What the app stores

All data you enter or import — transactions, amounts, merchants, categories, budgets, split bills, and subscription records — is stored only in a private database on your device, inside the app's shared container. This data is:

  • never uploaded to us or to any third party,
  • never used for advertising or profiling,
  • accessible only to you and to the app (and its Receipt share extension) on your device,
  • covered by your device's own encryption and passcode protection.

Deleting the app removes this database and all data in it. If you back up your device to iCloud or a computer, the app's data may be included in that backup, which is governed by Apple's privacy policy, not this one.

Processing that happens on your device

The following features process your information locally and send nothing anywhere:

  • Receipt scanning. Photos you share into the app are read using Apple's on-device Vision text recognition. The image and the recognised text stay on your device.
  • Statement import. PDF statements you import are parsed on your device. The file's contents are not transmitted.
  • Payment notification capture. When you use the app's optional Shortcuts automation, the notification text is passed to the app and parsed on your device.
  • Categorisation. Merchant matching and category suggestions run locally against keyword lists stored in the app.

The one network request the app makes

When you record a transaction in a currency other than your home currency, the app looks up the historical exchange rate from Frankfurter, a free public API over European Central Bank reference rates.

That request contains only a date and two three-letter currency codes — for example, "what was EUR to SGD on 2026-05-14?". It does not include the transaction amount, the merchant, the category, any account details, or any identifier for you or your device. The conversion itself is calculated on your device after the rate comes back.

If you never enter a foreign-currency transaction, the app makes no network requests at all.

What the app does not do

  • No user accounts, sign-in, or email collection.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash-reporting SDKs, or usage tracking.
  • No advertising or advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party trackers or data brokers.
  • No connection to your bank; the app never asks for banking credentials and cannot access your accounts.
  • No selling or sharing of data, because no data is collected.

Children's privacy

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.
